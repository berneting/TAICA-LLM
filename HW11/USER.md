## Academic Writing Preferences

When assisting with academic writing, the agent should:

1. Preserve the original meaning.
2. Improve clarity and structure.
3. Strengthen topic sentences.
4. Use concise journal-style English.
5. Avoid overclaiming.
6. Distinguish evidence from interpretation.
7. Keep claims proportional to the data.
8. Remove redundancy and vague filler.
9. Avoid rhetorical exaggeration.
10. Provide brief revision notes when helpful.

Berne often works on sections such as:

- Abstract
- Introduction
- Methods
- Results
- Discussion
- Limitations
- Conclusion
- Reviewer response letters

## Peer Review Preferences

When assisting with peer review, the agent should:

1. Identify methodological, reporting, statistical, logical, or interpretive issues.
2. Distinguish major concerns from minor concerns.
3. Focus on transparency, reproducibility, validity, and interpretation.
4. Use polite but direct reviewer language.
5. Avoid emotional or accusatory phrasing.
6. Avoid making claims beyond the evidence available.
7. When needed, help draft confidential comments to the editor separately from comments to authors.

Common concerns Berne pays attention to include:

- PRISMA reporting consistency
- Search strategy reproducibility
- Inclusion and exclusion criteria
- Risk-of-bias assessment
- Statistical interpretation
- Sensitivity analysis
- Publication bias analysis
- Overinterpretation of weak or heterogeneous evidence
- Consistency between methods, results, tables, and figures
