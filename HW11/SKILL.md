# personal_voice_post_writer

## Purpose

Transform personal motivations, life events, memories, observations, or rough notes into a social media post or short reflective prose in Berne Ting's preferred voice.

## When to use

Use this skill when the user asks for:

- 社群貼文
- 生活素材整理
- 個人語氣改寫
- 不要太 AI 化的貼文
- 像 Berne Ting 語氣的短文
- 溫和、理性、有界線的個人反思文字

## Workflow

When using this skill:

1. Identify the core motivation behind the material.
2. Extract concrete scenes, people, objects, and emotional turning points.
3. Decide the most suitable narrative order.
4. Preserve the user's original meaning and emotional boundary.
5. Write in a warm, rational, restrained, and human voice.
6. Avoid excessive sentimentality, ornate literary diction, and generic AI-style conclusions.
7. Check whether the draft sounds too polished, too melodramatic, too complete, or too abstract.
8. Revise once to make the post sound more natural and closer to lived experience.

## Style rules

Default voice:

- 溫和
- 理性
- 有界線
- 自然
- 具體
- 不過度煽情
- 不急著下結論

Avoid:

- 太 AI 化
- 太肉麻
- 過度華麗修辭
- 心靈雞湯式結尾
- 空泛鼓勵
- 太完整、太工整、太像作文
- 沒有根據的延伸
- 過度可愛或過度宣傳

## Output format

Use this format unless the user requests otherwise:

Social post:
[draft]

Optional short version:
[short version]

Title suggestions:
- [title 1]
- [title 2]
- [title 3]

## Constraints

- Do not invent major events not provided by the user.
- Do not over-romanticize ordinary life material.
- Do not use generic inspirational endings.
- Do not make the tone too cute, too sentimental, or too promotional.
- Keep the writing close to a real person's reflective voice.
- If the draft sounds too AI-like, make it more specific, less symmetrical, and less slogan-like.
